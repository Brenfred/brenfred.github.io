What do you do when you want something so badly but it seems to forever be just out of your grasp? For Barron “Bear” Bailey (Michael Johnston), the answer is simple: you wish for it. *Obsession* is a darkly funny, twisty horror flick from Youtuber Curry Barker takes a young incel and gives him the relationship that he thinks he wants. It doesn’t take long for things to go horribly wrong, and Barker takes the audience on a wild ride as we discover the consequences of Bear’s actions.

Bear is a typical “nice guy”. He takes care of his beloved cat, goes to bar trivia with his friends (who all work together at a record store conveniently staffed mostly by young, attractive people), and is always willing to give someone a ride home when they need it. His biggest problem is that he has a crush on his friend Nikki (Inde Navarrette), but he doesn’t know how to tell her about his feelings. In a scene that will be panic-inducing for anyone who has ever been through a similar situation, he is presented with opportunities again and again to tell Nikki how he feels about her. Bear chickens out every single time. As soon as she leaves he pulls out a “One-Wish Willow” in desperation, snaps it in half and wishes that she would love him more than anyone in the world. The consequences are immediate and dire: he has just made a decision that will set up the rest of the movie and alter the course of his life.

The star of the movie from this point forward is Nikki. She is possessed by some unexplained force (the rules of the magic in this movie are never explained, and that’s for the better) that makes her act out Bear’s wish literally, and she behaves in ways that are so unpredictable and extreme that Bear and the audience stay on the edge of our seats for the entire time. Screaming, covering herself in blood and goo, and moving her body in some of the most unsettling ways seen onscreen since Kiyoshi Kurosawa’s Pulse (2001), Navarrette leaves nothing on the table. The story stays unpredictable until the very end, due to Barker’s clever script and Navarrette’s intense, star-making performance. Look out for her name to pop up in casting for major projects soon!

If there is a lesson to be had here, it’s pretty simple: if you’re interested in someone, say so, and if they don’t reciprocate just move on. The fun to be had with this film is in exploring just how badly things can go wrong when an unrealistic wish becomes reality. Forming new relationships is difficult and complicated, and there is no cheat code that will let you skip to the good parts. If you’re not squeamish around blood and guts, there is one wish you can make that is guaranteed to come true, with no strings attached! You will have a great time watching this movie!
