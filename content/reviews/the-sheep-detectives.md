When legendary filmmaker Kyle Balda makes a new movie… you have to gather your herd and rush to the theater. This man has cooked up heaters like *Minions*, *Minions: The Rise of Gru*, and *Despicable Me 3* (which has Minions in jumpsuits), so you know Balda is always in his bag.

*The Sheep Detectives* marks his first directorial venture outside of the MCU (Minion Cinematic Universe), as well as his first live-action flick, and honestly, you may read the above as sarcasm or a joke, but this next sentence I truly mean with all of my heart: *The Sheep Detectives* may just be the best movie of the year so far. The 109-minute four-quadrant event of the summer stars Hugh Jackman as George Hardy, a shepherd by day and a reader of detective novels for his herd by night. George adores his found family, but one day, while trying to reconnect with his biological children, he’s found dead with hints of foul play after a mysterious will appears. After a clueless policeman is assigned to the case, George’s flock must step in to solve the mystery.

*The Sheep Detectives* perfectly blends everything you’d want from a film for the whole family. The film offers surprisingly sharp social commentary, especially considering what the trailer sells it as. Through its cast of farm animals, it dives into different forms of prejudice they experience, whether it’s based on the type of sheep they are, the age they are on the farm, or even the season they were born in. There’s an entire subplot about a winter lamb that honestly becomes one of the movie’s strongest aspects.

“The Sheep Detective” is also genuinely just a gripping whodunit that always feels multiple steps ahead of its audience, and a very underrated aspect of the movie is just how funny it is. The audience I saw it with was laughing nonstop, and it made the whole thing feel like such a communal experience.

But what really makes *The Sheep Detectives* special is the warmth the movie radiates and the emotional depth woven into every sequence. Beneath the clever mystery and the witty dialogue is a flick that wants to talk about the sorrows of loneliness, about how important it is to find your community, and how important having compassion can be, that emotional core really snuck up on me and completely enamoured me.

*The Sheep Detectives* is such a smart movie without ever feeling self-indulgent. It’s heartfelt without becoming overly sentimental, and above all else, it’s just an incredibly fun time at the theater. I went to see this movie with my significant other, but it feels like the kind of film you could have just as much fun seeing with a larger group of pals, with your family, or even as a lone wolf. Which is wild to say about a film centered around talking sheep trying to solve a murder, but it’s such a conversation starter and the kind of movie you just don’t want to stop talking about.

The comparison many will make is to *Knives Out*… but if Benoit Blanc were a pack of farm animals and somehow still carried the same razor-sharp mystery mechanics and ensemble charm. But even with me saying all of that, it still undersells just how unique *The Sheep Detectives*  ultimately is. This movie made me feel like that Martin Scorsese meme where his hands are up saying, “This is cinema.” I was completely overwhelmed by the visual and dynamic storytelling on display.

All of this combines to add another shiny star to Amazon MGM Studios’ 2026 slate so far. After *Project Hail Mary* captured the zeitgeist back in March and received unanimous praise from both audiences and critics, currently just having passed Oppenheimer’s domestic box office, and with their newest film *Is God Is* premiering with a 87 on Metacritic, the studio is on an absolute tear when we add *The Sheep Detectives* into the mix. While Warner Bros. may have taken a step back in 2026 after their glorifying run in 2025 headlined by films like *One Battle After Another*, *Sinners*, and *Weapons*, MGM’s mighty lion has stepped up (including the hilarious “BAHHH” as it roars during the film’s opening moments).

While I think some people will watch the trailer and assume the movie is just a cringe fest, I genuinely believe this could become the next *Paddington 2*… a film that initially looks like a charming family comedy but is so elevated that it ultimately lands with incredible emotional weight.

*The Sheep Detectives* will make you shed at least one tear … if not a lot, especially by the end. The film somehow miraculously makes you deeply attached to a group of CGI animals, and from the very opening of hearing George’s voice speak so eloquently about why he loves his flock to the final shot where a character is reading a novel, you are completely locked into all the dynamics that unfold in this mystery. This is a movie where the wit absolutely is on my wavelength, and the heart radiates off the screen and is pumping in my chest. The entire experience leaves you walking out of the theater reminded why you fell in love with movies in the first place. It’s a film you want to recommend to your friends, your family, your colleagues. *The Sheep Detectives* makes you want to go back and rewatch and discuss with those around. So yes, this 10 out of 10 is completely sincere.
